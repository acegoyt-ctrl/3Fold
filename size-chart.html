How to pick your size

  1. Lay a tee you already like flat on a table.
  2. Width: measure straight across the chest, from one side seam to the other.
  3. Length: measure from the top of the shoulder, next to the collar, down to the bottom hem.
  4. Sleeve: measure from the shoulder seam to the end of the sleeve.
  5. Compare your numbers with the chart. For a looser look, go one size up.

All measurements are taken with the shirt laid flat, so small differences can happen between pieces. Not sure which size to get? Message us and we'll help.
